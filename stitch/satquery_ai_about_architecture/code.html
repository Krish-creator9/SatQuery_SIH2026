System Limitations & Clarifications

No Predictive Guarantee

SatQuery AI analyzes historical and current data states. It does not provide guaranteed disaster prediction or future state modeling.

Not Legal Verification

Findings regarding construction or land usage are observational. They do not constitute legally binding verification or zoning compliance assessments.

Sensor Limitations

Optical analysis is subject to atmospheric interference (cloud cover). SAR provides all-weather capability but lacks spectral material identification.
